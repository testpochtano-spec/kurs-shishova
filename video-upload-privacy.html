Video_Upload Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

1. Information We Use

Video_Upload uses TikTok authorization data only to connect the creator's TikTok account and publish content that the creator chooses to export.

2. TikTok Data

The app may request basic TikTok account information and creator posting information required by TikTok's Content Posting API, such as available privacy options and interaction settings.

3. Local Storage

Access tokens and configuration files are stored locally on the creator's computer for the purpose of authenticated publishing. They are not sold or shared with third parties.

4. Video Content

Video files selected by the creator are uploaded to TikTok only as part of the creator-confirmed export flow. Video_Upload does not use uploaded content for advertising or profiling.

5. Third Parties

TikTok processes data according to TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. Video_Upload is not affiliated with TikTok.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
